# Quickstart Guide

This guide will walk you through the basic functionality of the Discipline & Self-Improvement Tracker.

## 1. Setting Your Goals

Before you start tracking, you need to set your daily goals. Navigate to the 'Wellness & Development' tab and set your targets for:

-   Calories
-   Protein, Carbs, and Fats
-   Cognitive Games (e.g., 30 minutes)
-   French Practice (e.g., 20 minutes)
-   News Reading (e.g., 3 articles)

## 2. Tracking Your Day

As you go through your day, log your activities:

-   **Gym:** Check the box when you've completed your workout.
-   **Water:** Use the `+` button to add to your water intake.
-   **Food:** Log your meals to track calories and macros.
-   **Cognitive Games & French Practice:** Start the timer when you begin a session. The timer will run, and you can pause or stop it as needed.
-   **News Reading:** Log the articles you've read.

## 3. Monitoring Your Progress

-   **Streaks:** The streak counter in the top navigation bar will increment every day you meet all your goals.
-   **Charts:** The application provides various charts to visualize your progress over time.
-   **AI Insights:** Navigate to the 'AI Insights' tab to ask questions about your performance and get personalized recommendations.
